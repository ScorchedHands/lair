Verdict

Candidate B. Effective amortization plateaus far below stated row width (factor 4.9–6.1 at t8 for every kernel whether w32 or w64; ≤24 vs theoretical 64 even at best posture), doubling the ceiling gains only −10%…+70%, and implied per-pass bandwidth roughly halves when width doubles (e.g., gemv t8: impliedWeightGB/s=0.93 → 0.31). Widening MaxPrefillRowWidth alone will not deliver a near-proportional gain; kernel grouping must be restructured first so each traversal actually amortizes its rows.

Deviation note: per-token baselines ran at reps=1 (per-token cost ≈0.5 s/token t8, ≈0.75 s/token t32 made spec's reps=3 impractical — one aborted r3 attempt kept as evidence). Fit lines for arm2 gemv w32 and arm4 gemv w64 carry engine-reported UNRELIABLE markers; their tok/s values are still used. No kernel refusals occurred on any span.
