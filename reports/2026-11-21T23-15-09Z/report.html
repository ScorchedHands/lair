Metric naming: “gather-step tok/s” is the merged-decode DecodeBatch union-gather rate on a pinned synthetic qwen2moe F32 fixture (8 sequences × 24 tokens × 3 timed reps = 576 sequence-tokens per arm). It is not end-to-end token generation for Flash-Next or any flagship model.

Gather-step tok/s comparison

Parity

All four runs: gathered-vs-dense-equivalent logits bit-exact (49,152 floats compared per arm), and gather-vs-ram-hot-tier bit-exact in arms 2–4. Residency changed where slab bytes come from, never the arithmetic over them.

Gate verdict

  • Closed — validated on a streaming substrate: with real expensive reads available to save against, RamHotExpertTier as built delivers +72.1% gather-step rate at half budget (45.8% hits) and converts the entire timed-rep expert stream into zero storage reads at ceiling budget (344.9 tok/s vs 6.5–17.3 for streaming prefetch-only).
  • The earlier null result is explained, not contradicted: the mmap control reproduces it exactly — identical residency accounting with −10.2% tok/s because OS-cached pages leave no expensive read for residency to save.

Lane caveats and blockers (see manifest.json / host.json for full diagnostics)

  • Synthetic qwen2moe fixture: proves substrate behavior of the residency seam, not flagship-model throughput. Pinned Flash-Next tensors were not measured because on the Xeon lane they live in tmpfs (RAM), where eviction cannot create an expensive-read baseline.
  • Xeon CPU-only lane blocked for this measurement: /tmp is tmpfs (128 GiB; pinned models ~71 GiB RAM-resident); disk-backed ZFS root sits outside the sanctioned /tmp-only scratch rule. dotnet not on PATH (runtime 10.0.15 present at /usr/local/dotnet, SDK path unestablished). No perf binary and perf_event_paranoid=4, so LLC-load-miss/stall counters were unavailable without privileged host changes.
  • Windows SEC_NOCACHE mapping variant rejected by the OS (CreateFileMapping(PAGE_READWRITE|SEC_NOCACHE) → win32 193, ERROR_BAD_EXE_FORMAT from the SEC_IMAGE alias) and was dropped; FILE_FLAG_NO_BUFFERING works only when no cached mapping competes for the same bytes.
  • No Xeon NUMA/LLC fidelity in these numbers: this is a local NVMe streaming substrate with honest page-fault tallies, not flagship-model flagship hardware evidence. Flagship/Xeon confirmation of the gate remains open until either a disk-backed scratch decision or an SDK+perf setup on that host is authorized.
