Reading

  • The old ~6.5x sub-4-bit penalty is not present on this build; measured stable per-element costs are roughly 0.25–0.39 ns/elt against the Q4_K baseline, i.e. about 1.5–2.3x depending on format.
  • IQ3_XXS modestly exceeds the intended ~1–2x band at about 2.3x on this posture; recorded rather than smoothed.
  • IQ4_XS is the fastest measured live fixture (~16.5 monitor-windowed decode tok/s), ahead of Q4_K_M (~12.4) and the mixed-tier sub-4-bit file (~13.0), while staying under the 4 GB working-set ceiling.
  • Server startup selection varied across processes: corrected quota8 runs resolved gather for Q4_K_M/UD and scalar for IQ4_XS; live measurements report actual mode rather than one global encoding.
