Interpretation

  • Comparable-route formats (Q8_0, Q4_K, IQ4_XS) are the only direct CPU-blocked-matmul comparisons; CUDA lands near tie or slower against auto-threaded CPU blocked matmul (0.90, 0.83, 0.79).
  • Fallback-route wins (Q5_0, Q6_K, Q5_K, Q3_K) are not whole-prefill claims because attention and device-resident KV still cross the seam per layer.
  • Unquantized F32/F16/BF16 show total-pass losses against auto-threaded CPU batched-row weight passes despite faster kernel-busy.
  • Largest relative drift is IQ4_XS (0.00898242, max absolute drift 0.0546875); drift values do not assert or widen parity bands.
  • No automatic GEMM default and no cuBLAS/cuBLASLt escalation promoted from this bench.
