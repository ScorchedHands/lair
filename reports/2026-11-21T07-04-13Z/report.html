Expert-gather observations

  • Mainline b12032: n_expert=512, n_expert_used=10, n_expert_groups=0.
  • Official ik main: fused_moe=1, graph nodes=4750, graph splits=1, "enabling only_active_experts scheduling".
  • Neither harness logged per-layer selected expert ids; LAIR gather design will rely on the recorded geometry (512 total / 10 active) rather than harness expert traces.

Gate verdict

  • Official pinned ik_llama.cpp cannot run the target model (segfault/FPE) — rejected as a baseline harness.
  • Official main ik_llama.cpp llama-bench is the usable ik baseline: pp128 ≈ 24 tok/s, tg256 ≈ 4.3 tok/s.
  • Mainline llama.cpp b12032 baseline: pp128 ≈ 13.9–14.9 tok/s, tg256 ≈ 3.2–3.3 tok/s.
  • ik_llama.cpp main is ~1.7× mainline on prefill and ~1.3× on decode on this Xeon — the MoE-specific optimizations (only_active_experts scheduling, fused MoE) are the visible source of the gap.
  • CUDA observations remain deferred to a scripted idle-accelerator run per DN-M7-13.
