Notes

  • Arms simulate layer-2 access scheduling over the real table's row addressing with a synthetic token stream; production qwen4exp forward integration is not implemented and no claim here covers it.
  • The stream uses uniform token ids (uniform ids across 1e6..2e6, the no-skew control) with EOS cut and missing-predecessor windows mixed in; policy comparisons hold this stream identical across arms.
  • hot-resident-prefetch versus hot-resident-no-prefetch isolates what staying resident buys from what prefetch overlap buys; demand-only is the floor of both.
